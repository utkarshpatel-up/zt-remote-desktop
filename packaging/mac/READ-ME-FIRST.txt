ZT Remote Desktop for macOS (Apple Silicon and Intel)

1. Drag "ZT Remote Desktop" into Applications.
2. First time only: right-click (or Control-click) the app > Open > Open.
   (The app is not signed by Apple, so a plain double-click is refused once.)
3. A Terminal window opens with the address and access password to use on
   your phone.
4. Grant Terminal these permissions (System Settings > Privacy & Security),
   then quit and start the app again:
     - Screen Recording
     - Accessibility

Updating: download the new .dmg and drag the app into Applications again
(replace). Your password and settings are kept in
~/Library/Application Support/ZT Remote Desktop.
